## 📡 Math. Not Guesswork.

Marketing is too expensive to manage manually, but too critical to hand over to an autonomous AI. **adCasa** is the **AI Marketing OS** engineered specifically for performance operators and scaling brands.

It securely stores creatives, connects your live ad channels, runs Media Mix Modeling (Meridian) to uncover true incremental ROI, and provides a persistent workspace where every automated financial action requires explicit human approval.

> [!NOTE]
> **The 3 Laws of our OS:**
> 1. **Data Over Intuition:** Human bias destroys budgets. Every action proposed by the system must be backed by causal inference math.
> 2. **Automation Over Administration:** If a task requires manual spreadsheet syncing or moving CSVs, it is a vulnerability. We engineer it out of existence.
> 3. **Profit Over Vanity:** The engine targets net revenue and down-funnel conversion truth—not vanity clicks or impressions.

### Bayesian Causal Inference Architecture
Our engine leverages Google's **Meridian MMM** framework to calculate true incremental uplift across complex multi-touch attribution funnels:

* **Adstock Decay:** Models media saturation and residual brand impact using geometric and Weibull decay functions.
* **Hill Transformed Curves:** Prevents diminishing returns by dynamically detecting ad spend saturation thresholds.
* **Counterfactual Baselines:** Calculates what revenue would have occurred *without* paid media spend to eliminate organic cannibalization.
